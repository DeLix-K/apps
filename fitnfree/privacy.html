FitNFree Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Privacy Policy Terms & Conditions Delete Account

Team K ("we," "us," "our") built FitNFree (the "App"), an AI-powered fitness, nutrition, sleep, and wellness companion. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you use FitNFree, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By using FitNFree, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

FitNFree deals with health-related data. We only collect what the App's features actually need to function, we never sell your data, and most of your data (workouts, sleep, mood, habits) is stored privately and is readable only by you, enforced at the database level, not just in the app's UI.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your email address and password (the password is stored securely hashed by our authentication provider — we never see or store it in plain text). You may optionally add a display name.

Health & fitness data

To provide the App's core features, we store the data you generate while using them: logged workouts, sets and duration, workout plans, streaks and challenge participation, habit check-ins, mood check-ins and journal notes, sleep logs (entered manually or synced from Oura, if you connect it), body weight/profile stats used to calculate calorie and protein targets, your Body log (weight, body fat and measurements you enter), and your history of AI Coach conversations and AI feature usage (exercise explanations, nutrition lookups, scan results). Your personal records and badges are worked out on your phone from the sets you log; we do not store them as a separate record. You can download a copy of what you have logged at any time from Profile → Export my data.

Body log and progress photos

Weight, body fat and measurements you add to the Body log are stored in your account and are visible only to you. Progress photos you add are stored only on your device. We never receive or upload them, and they are not sent to our servers or to any AI service. Because they live only on your phone, they are removed if you delete the app, and we cannot recover them. If your phone is included in your own iCloud or computer backup, that backup belongs to you and we do not receive it.

Reminders, widget and Apple Watch

Workout reminders and the rest timer use notifications that your phone schedules on the device itself; your reminder settings are stored only on your device and we do not send you push notifications from a server. The iPhone home-screen widget and the Apple Watch app show your streak, which days this week you trained, your weekly target and whether you trained today. This information moves only between your own iPhone and Apple Watch (the watch app can ask your phone to log today's workout); it is not sent to us for these features, and the watch app has no internet access of its own. Signing out clears it from the widget and the watch.

Coaching check-ins with a trainer

If you choose to link with a trainer using an invite code the trainer gives you, the answers you send in a check-in (for example weight, energy, sleep, stress and notes) are shared with that trainer only, together with any messages you exchange with them in the App. The trainer sees the name they typed for you, not your account details. You can stop sharing at any time, after which the trainer can no longer see your check-ins (past ones too), and you can delete your check-ins. They are deleted when you delete your account. If you are a trainer, the check-in forms you create and the check-ins your linked clients send you are stored in your account.

Friends (optional)

Friends is off until you turn it on. If you turn it on, other FitNFree members who also turned it on can find you by your display name and send you a friend request. Only people you accept see your updates, and only the kinds you switch on: that you logged a workout, a streak milestone, or a personal record on an exercise from the App's library. Your display name is the only text of yours that friends see; there are no posts or comments. You can turn each kind off, delete anything you shared, or leave Friends at any time, which deletes your friendships, updates and cheers. You can block or report anyone. We do not use this for advertising.

Trainer profiles

If you register as a trainer, the profile you create (such as your display name, bio, specialties and prices, and any photos or videos you add to it) is visible to other FitNFree users so they can find and book you.

Photos and camera

If you use equipment scan, food photo scan, or AI form check, the App accesses your camera or photo library only when you actively choose to use those features. The photo you submit is sent to our AI provider (see "Third-party service providers" below) to generate a result and is recorded in your in-app scan history; we do not otherwise store or repurpose your photos.

Microphone / voice

If you use voice input with the AI Coach, speech recognition happens using your device's on-device capabilities. We do not store audio recordings — only the resulting transcribed text is sent as your message.

AI features: your permission

Before FitNFree sends anything to our AI provider (Anthropic) for the first time, it asks for your permission in the app. If you say no, nothing is sent and the AI features stay off. If you say yes, what you ask the AI Coach, photos you submit for scans, and relevant profile and log information (such as goals, workouts, nutrition, sleep and mood entries) are sent to Anthropic to generate replies. You can change your choice at any time in Profile under "AI data sharing".

Reports about content

If you report a challenge, leaderboard name, squad or trainer profile, we store your report (who reported, what was reported and your reason) so our team can review it. Reports are visible only to us.

Location

If you grant location permission, we use it to support outdoor activity tracking (e.g. logging an outdoor run, walk or ride). Location is used only while the App is open on screen, not in the background. The route of an activity you track is saved to your account so you can see it again, and is visible only to you. Location is not collected unless you grant this permission, and you can revoke it at any time in your device settings.

Payment information

Purchases of the Premium subscription made in the iPhone or iPad app are processed by Apple (the App Store), and on Android by Google (Google Play). Apple and Google handle your payment details; we receive only what we need to confirm your subscription (for example whether it is active and when it renews). Other purchases (one-time digital products, courses, merch, trainer plans) and Premium bought on the web are processed by Stripe. We never receive or store your full card number — Stripe handles payment details directly and shares with us only what's needed to fulfil and support your order (e.g. purchase status, order type, shipping address for merch).

Wearable data (optional)

If you choose to connect an Oura Ring account, we receive sleep, readiness, and activity data from Oura's API to power the Sleep and Habits features. This only happens if you explicitly connect your account, and you can disconnect it at any time in the App.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, operate, and personalize the App's features (workout/nutrition/sleep/wellness tracking, the AI Coach, streaks and challenges, the trainer marketplace, courses, and merch store)
  • To process purchases and subscriptions, and to fulfil merch orders
  • To generate AI responses (coaching messages, scan results, insights) via our AI provider
  • To maintain the security and integrity of your account and our systems
  • To communicate with you about your account, orders, or support requests

We do not use your health data for advertising, and FitNFree does not integrate any third-party advertising or ad-tracking SDK.

4. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, so the App can keep working the way you expect. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete Account in the App. This is self-service and takes effect immediately; it cannot be undone. You can also email us at teamlix6@gmail.com to request deletion instead.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. You can exercise most of these directly in the App (edit your profile, delete individual logs, download a copy of your data from Profile → Export my data, or delete your account outright); for anything else, contact us at teamlix6@gmail.com and we'll respond within a reasonable time.

6. Children's privacy

FitNFree is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data security

Your data is protected in transit via encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by our database provider. Access to your personal health data (workouts, sleep, mood, habits, AI history) is restricted at the database level so that only your own authenticated account can read it — this is enforced by row-level security policies, not just by app-side checks. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where this happens, those providers are responsible for maintaining appropriate safeguards under their own privacy and security commitments.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email teamlix6@gmail.com.
